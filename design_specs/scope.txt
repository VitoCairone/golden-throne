There will be SEVEN provinces including the starting province.

The Story Mode should be around 90 minutes to clear each area,
ergo around 11 hours to complete Story Mode.

There should be an option for a Quick Match which lasts about 1 hour.

When the Villain is defeated, the player with the most Heroics is the Winner.

Heroics are earned mainly for rescuing Towns, and also for completing Missions.

The stack for the project is
  Ruby on Rails (API-first backend)
  PostgreSQL (persistent data)
  Redis (turn state and locking)
  JavaScript frontend (DOM-based MVP)

STATS
  AT (Attack)
  DF (Defence)
  MG (Magic)
  SP (Speed)
  TO (Toughness)
